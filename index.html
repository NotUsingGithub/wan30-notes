Draft cheap, render the keeper once

Most of the cost in AI video is not the idea. It is re-rendering a shot you already know is wrong. I started treating the first pass as a storyboard: short clips, lower resolution, no upscale, and I throw away anything that fails the first three seconds.

On a recent batch of product shots I logged 18 drafts and kept 2. The discarded drafts were the expensive habit. A 5-second low-res pass is enough to see motion, hands, and whether the prompt actually followed the shot list. Only the keepers go to a full render.

Compared with generating everything at final quality, that split cut my failed-render spend by roughly the share of drafts I throw away, which in this log was about 16 of 18. It is not a model benchmark. It is just how I stop paying full price for clips I will never ship.

The tool I am using for that draft-then-render loop is the Wan 3.0 video generator.
